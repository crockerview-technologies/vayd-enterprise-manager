# TSK-02. Make a task for someone

- **Audience:** CLs, techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-01.
- **Signed in as:** Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **The one thing to remember:** pick a person when you know who should do it. Leave it unassigned only when anyone on that team can.

## Why (opening)

"When something needs doing and you aren't the one to do it, make a task. It lands on that person's list with everything they need, instead of in a hallway conversation."

## What else you can do here

- **When to start** can be a time from now ("2 weeks from now") instead of Today; **Start** and **Due** can be typed.
- **Linked to** can be a client instead of a pet.
- **New Task** in the left menu opens the same form from any page.
- After saving, **Edit** on the task changes anything, including the title and links.

## What other roles see

- Everyone sees the same form. Watchers can see the task but only the assignee, the person who made it, or an admin can close or edit it.
- The form makes one person's task. A task shared by several people (one copy each) is only made by Scout itself, for example the labs-pending task (EMR Help topic).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: leaving Assign to blank, so the task sits on a queue nobody watches, and cramming the instructions into the title. Remember: pick a person when you know who should do it."
