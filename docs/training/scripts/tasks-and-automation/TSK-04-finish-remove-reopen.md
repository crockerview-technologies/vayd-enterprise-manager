# TSK-04. Finish, remove or reopen a task

- **Audience:** CLs, techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-01.
- **Signed in as:** Jordan Pike (technician).
- **The one thing to remember:** **Mark done** means the work is done. **×** (Remove) means it no longer needs doing, and you say why.

## Why (opening)

"A task leaves your list in one of two ways: the work got done, or it no longer needs doing. Scout records which, so the history tells the truth."

## What else you can do here

- **Just me** in the shared window takes it off only your list.
- The detail page has the same **Remove** button (top right) as the card's **×**.
- Reason chips: No longer needed, Handled another way, Created by mistake, Duplicate of another task, Client declined.

## What other roles see

- Only the person it's assigned to, whoever made it, or an admin can mark it done, remove it or reopen it. A watcher sees "You are watching this task. Only the assignee, creator, or an admin can edit or complete it."
- Callbacks have no **Mark done**: they close with **Chart & complete** (TSK-06).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: marking a task done when you've only started it, and removing a task just because you can't get to it. Use Move to hand it on instead. Remember: Mark done means the work is done; Remove means it no longer needs doing."
