# TSK-14. See someone else's tasks, and move them when they're out

- **Audience:** managers with an admin login.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-05.
- **Signed in as:** Dr. Avery Lane's admin login.
- **The one thing to remember:** use **Person** to open their list, then move what's due and what's coming.

## Why (opening)

"When someone is out sick or on leave, their tasks don't wait politely. An admin can open anyone's list and hand the work to someone who's in."

## What else you can do here

- Someone leaving for good: **Settings → Staff → Offboard** moves all their open tasks, and their future automatic tasks, to one person (Help topic "Offboard someone who is leaving").
- **Watching**, **Sent** and **Completed** also follow the Person box.

## What other roles see

- Practice managers get **Person** too, for people who share a branch with them; they can move those people's tasks and open them read-only (Q5, Nov 13).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: moving only the overdue tasks and forgetting what's coming, and leaving no note, so the new owner has to guess. Remember: use Person to open their list, then move what's due and what's coming."
