# TSK-06. Work a callback task

- **Audience:** CLs, techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-01; EMR V6 (adding callbacks at wrap-up).
- **Signed in as:** Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **The one thing to remember:** a callback is finished only when the conversation is filed to the chart.

## Why (opening)

"A callback is a promise to a family that we'll check in. Working it from the task keeps the call, the notes and the chart entry in one place."

## What else you can do here

- **Chart & re-assign** files what you have and hands the rest to someone else.
- **Remove** takes the callback off without filing anything.
- **+ Task → Callback** makes a callback by hand; the Wrap-up page makes them at the end of a visit (EMR V6).

## What other roles see

- Everyone files callbacks to the pet's chart as a communication note; a medical note still needs a doctor or licensed tech.
- Callbacks have no **Mark done** button on the list.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: closing a callback without filing what was said, and filing a household's call to the wrong pet. Remember: a callback is finished only when the conversation is filed to the chart."
