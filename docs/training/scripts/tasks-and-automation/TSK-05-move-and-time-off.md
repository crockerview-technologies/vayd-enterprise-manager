# TSK-05. Move a task, and hand off before time off

- **Audience:** CLs, techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 2 minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-04.
- **Signed in as:** Jordan Pike (technician).
- **The one thing to remember:** before time off, open **Future** and click **Re-assign all on this tab**.

## Why (opening)

"Plans change. The job belongs to someone else, or it can't start until next week, or you're about to be away. Move hands a task on without losing its notes or history."

## What else you can do here

- **Assign to: Queue (unassigned)** in Move puts a task back on a team's queue.
- The task page has the same **Move** button (top right), and **Edit** for the title, notes and links.

## What other roles see

- **Active** and **Expired** have the same bulk buttons, for handing off what's already due (Q4, Nov 13).
- Admins can do this for someone else from the **Person** box (TSK-14).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: leaving for time off with tasks still in Future, and moving a task with no note, so the next person has to guess. Remember: before time off, open Future and click Re-assign all on this tab."
