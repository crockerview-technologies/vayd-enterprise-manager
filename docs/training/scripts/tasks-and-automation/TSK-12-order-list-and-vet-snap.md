# TSK-12. The order-list task and the weekly Vet Snap task

- **Audience:** inventory managers and the practice manager.
- **Length:** 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-07; the inventory videos (order list, Vet Snap log).
- **Signed in as:** Jordan Pike (the Raleigh office's Inventory Manager), then Robin Hale (practice manager).
- **The one thing to remember:** the order-list task opens your office's order list, and the Vet Snap task closes as soon as anyone downloads the file.

## Why (opening)

"Two automatic tasks keep the pharmacy on schedule: the order list, so items get ordered, and the weekly Vet Snap upload, so our controlled-drug patients are reported."

## What else you can do here

- **Download CSV** and **Print log** on Controlled Drugs (inventory videos).
- An order list with items at two offices makes one task per office.

## What other roles see

- Order-list tasks go to the Inventory Manager **for that office**. With two at one office, the task waits on the Inventory Manager queue for that office (TA-A3). The practice manager watches every one.
- The Vet Snap task goes to the practice manager; the download needs `controlled.vetsnap_export`.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: marking the order-list task done before the order is placed, and downloading the Vet Snap file without uploading it. Remember: the order-list task opens your office's order list, and the Vet Snap task closes as soon as anyone downloads the file."
