# TSK-03. Pick up work from the Unassigned queue

- **Audience:** CLs and techs.
- **Length:** 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-01.
- **Signed in as:** Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **The one thing to remember:** click **Assign to me** before you start, so two people don't do the same work.

## Why (opening)

"Some work isn't meant for one person. A new family's question can go to the whole client liaison team, and the first one free takes it. That's the Unassigned tab."

## What else you can do here

- Open a queue card first to read it, then **Assign to me** at the top of the task.
- To give queue work to someone else, use **Move** (TSK-05).

## What other roles see

- Techs see the Technician queue, doctors the Veterinarian queue, and so on: **Show** lists only your roles' queues.
- Admin logins see every queue.
- Every unassigned task appears under **Urgent** on this tab (see the review file, question Q3).

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: working a queue task without assigning it to yourself first, and grabbing more than you can finish. If you can't get to it, Move it back to the queue. Remember: click Assign to me before you start."
