# TSK-11. "Enter results for …": an in-house lab with no results

- **Audience:** techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 1½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** TSK-07; LV-01 (in-house labs).
- **Signed in as:** Jordan Pike (technician).
- **The one thing to remember:** **Save results** closes the task. **Save, finish later** keeps it open.

## Why (opening)

"An in-house lab that was charged but never resulted is a hole in the record, and a question the family will ask. At six p.m., Scout gives whoever charged the lab a task to enter the results."

## What else you can do here

- Results entered from the visit's bill or **Labs → In-house labs** close the task too.
- IDEXX results have their own task and video (LV-06).

## What other roles see

- Client liaisons see the form read-only (no Save buttons): entering results needs `lab_result.enter`.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: leaving a lab on Save, finish later for days, and clicking Mark done without entering anything, which brings the task back. Remember: Save results closes the task; Save, finish later keeps it open."
