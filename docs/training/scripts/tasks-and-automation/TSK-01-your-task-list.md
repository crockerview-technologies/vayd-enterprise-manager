# TSK-01. Your task list: tabs, badges, Urgent and Normal

- **Audience:** CLs, techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 2½ minutes.
- **Watch first:** none.
- **Signed in as:** Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **The one thing to remember:** start every day on **Expired**, then work **Active**.

## Why (opening)

"Tasks is Scout's to-do list. A call back, a records request, a bill to close: it all lands here, so nothing depends on memory or sticky notes."

## What else you can do here

- **+ Task** (top left), or **New Task** in the left menu, makes a task (TSK-02).
- **Assign to me** on an Unassigned card (TSK-03).
- On the Unassigned tab, **Show** picks one of your queues.

## What other roles see

- Techs and doctors see the same page.
- Admin logins also get a **Person** box to open anyone's list (TSK-14).
- The Tasks page has no role limits: everyone sees only their own tasks, their queues and what they watch.

## Closing

"The most common mistakes: working only the Active tab and missing what's overdue, and letting the red number climb for days. Remember: start every day on Expired, then work Active."
