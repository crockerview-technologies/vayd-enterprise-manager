# TSK-15. The most common task mistakes

- **Audience:** CLs, techs and doctors.
- **Length:** 1 minute.
- **Watch first:** TSK-01 to TSK-07.
- **Signed in as:** Casey Morgan (client liaison).
- **The one thing to remember:** do the work, then close the task, and keep your own list current.

## Why (opening)

"A few habits keep tasks working for everyone. Here are the mistakes we see most, and the fix for each."
